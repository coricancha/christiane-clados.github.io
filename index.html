Scientific Illustration & Digital Reconstruction

Making archaeological and anthropological knowledge visible.

I develop scientific illustrations and digital reconstructions for research projects, museums, publications and exhibitions, with a particular focus on the material and visual cultures of the ancient Americas.

Combining anthropological and archaeological expertise with digital visualization, my work translates complex material and visual evidence into carefully grounded visual representations.

Research

Indigenous visual communication and epistemologies of material and visual culture in the ancient Americas.

My research explores Indigenous visual systems, graphic communication and material culture, particularly in the Central Andes and neighbouring regions.

I investigate how visual forms articulate knowledge, relationships and ways of understanding the world, including relations between humans, other-than-human beings and the environment.

My work brings together visual anthropology, iconographic analysis, material culture studies and postcolonial approaches.

Methods & Approaches

Visual and material analysis · Iconographic and comparative approaches · Digital reconstruction · Scientific visualization · 3D modelling · Photogrammetry · Experimental methods

Digital tools support the investigation and visualization of material evidence; they complement rather than replace anthropological and interpretive research.

Academic Profile

Christiane Clados is a social and cultural anthropologist specializing in Indigenous visual communication and material cultures of the ancient Americas. Her work connects anthropological research with visual analysis, digital methods and the epistemic dimensions of material culture.

Available for selected collaborations, scientific illustration commissions, digital reconstruction projects and academic exchange.
