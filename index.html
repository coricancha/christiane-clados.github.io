Scientific Illustration & Digital Reconstruction

Scientific illustration and digital reconstruction for research, museums, publications and exhibitions, with a focus on the visual and material cultures of the ancient Americas.

My work combines archaeological and anthropological research with digital visualization and experimental approaches to material and visual evidence.

Research

My research explores Indigenous visual communication, material culture and systems of knowledge in the Central Andes and neighbouring regions.

I am particularly interested in visual epistemologies, graphic communication, human–environment relations and the reconstruction of Indigenous perspectives through interdisciplinary and digital methods.

Methods & Areas of Interest

Visual anthropology · Iconographic analysis · Material culture · Digital reconstruction · Scientific visualization · Photogrammetry · 3D modelling
